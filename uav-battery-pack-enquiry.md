## What to send us / 询价需要哪些信息

- Aircraft type and application / 机型及用途
- Series count and target capacity / 串数及目标容量
- Continuous and peak current, and how long each is needed / 持续和峰值电流及持续时间
- Battery compartment dimensions and weight limit / 电池仓尺寸及重量限制
- Connector, charging and BMS requirements / 接插件、充电及 BMS 需求
- Quantity, destination and requested timing / 数量、目的地与计划时间

Not sure of every parameter? Send what you have and say **“selection needed”**. Configuration, quotation, availability and delivery arrangements are confirmed for the selected project; overseas battery transport must be checked for the destination.

参数未定也可以先咨询。具体配置、报价、供货与交付按项目确认；海外电池运输按目的地核实。
